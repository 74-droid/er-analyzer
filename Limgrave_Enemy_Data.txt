METHODOLOGY NOTES:
─────────────────
1. All data extracted from the ER Analyzer enemies.json datamined dataset.
2. "Unique Conceptual Attacks" are collapsed from raw behavior entries using:
   - Elemental buff variants (Fire/Magic/Lightning/Holy versions of same animation)
     count as 1 attack with noted elemental variants.
   - Directional variants (Left/Right) of the same move count as 1 attack.
   - Multi-hit combo parts count as 1 attack with noted hit count.
3. Absorption values: < 1.0 = resistant (takes less damage), 1.0 = neutral,
   > 1.0 = weak (takes more damage). Shown as multiplier and % reduction.
4. spAttribute on attacks indicates elemental/status effects:
   0=None, 10=Magic, 11=Fire, 12=Lightning, 13=Holy, 21=Rot, 22=Frost,
   24=Blood, 25=Madness
5. Some enemies (Wolf, Stray, Runebear, Omen, Grafted Scion) are tagged as boss
   in the dataset despite also appearing as field enemies. For these, the lowest-HP
   variant is used as a proxy for the field version, and this is noted.
6. Enemies appearing in multiple sub-regions are fully detailed in their primary
   location and cross-referenced in subsequent sections.
